Chocolate Chip Cookies

Warm, soft, and gooey chocolate chip cookies that are perfect for beginners and baking lovers.

Ingredients:

  • Flour
  • Sugar
  • Butter
  • Chocolate chips
  • Eggs

Steps:

  1. Preheat oven to 350°F
  2. Mix wet ingredients
  3. Add dry ingredients
  4. Fold in chocolate chips
  5. Bake for 10–12 minutes
